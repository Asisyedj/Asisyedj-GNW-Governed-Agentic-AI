Build a world-class, governed artificial intelligence platform that provides reliable, secure, and auditable access to frontier models through a local Perplexity bridge, enabling anti-gravity engineering workflows with full cryptographic accountability.

## Core Purpose
Operate a self-healing, locally-hosted bridge to Perplexity's model catalog that:
- Enforces zero-bypass routing through approved endpoints only
- Maintains cryptographic audit chains for all model interactions
- Provides circuit-breaker protected fallback routing across 99+ models

- Enables continuous health verification and automated recovery

- Supervisor achieves HEALTHY state within 60s of startup
- Cross-language wire compatibility (Python/TypeScript/Rust)
