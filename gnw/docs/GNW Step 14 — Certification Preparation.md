# GNW Step 14 — Certification Preparation

**Status:** Certification preparation framework documented. GNW is not certified by this document or by self-authored technical evidence.

## Assurance tracks

For ISO/IEC 27001, define the Information Security Management System scope, risk method, statement of applicability, access control, supplier management, continuity, incident handling, internal audit, management review, and corrective-action process. For ISO/IEC 42001, define the AI Management System scope, intended use, affected stakeholders, AI risk and impact assessment, human oversight, transparency, monitoring, incident handling, and continual improvement.

Map the technical evidence to NIST AI RMF, NIST SSDF, OWASP agentic-risk guidance, SBOM/provenance practices, and applicable legal obligations. Keep “audit-ready,” “readiness assessed,” and “certified” as separate statuses.

## External authority

ISO certification requires an accredited certification body. SOC 2 requires an independent CPA firm. EU AI Act obligations require product-role and deployment-specific legal analysis. GNW must not publish certification or conformity claims before the relevant external authority issues them.

## Final state

The project has a structured hardening and assurance path, but its overall release remains DENY until production evidence, operations, and independent review are complete.
